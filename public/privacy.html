Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Uma Boron is an independent cultural web experience created by Soham Roy. This policy explains what information the service uses and why.

Account information

If you create an account, Supabase Auth stores your email address, authentication identity, and the minimum account metadata needed to provide sign-in. If you use Google sign-in, Google shares your basic profile and email after you give consent. Uma Boron requests only the email, profile, and openid scopes.

Email-password fallback

After Google sign-up, you may create an email password for the same account. Passwords are processed and securely hashed by Supabase; Uma Boron cannot read your password. Never reuse a password from another service.

Location and contacts

Location is requested only when you choose to find nearby pandals or local weather. Coordinates are used in your browser to make those requests and are not stored in the Uma Boron database. Contact access is initiated by you, and only the contact you select is placed into the form on your device; the address book is not uploaded.

Memories and invitations

Signed-in users may choose to save festival memories or participate in private invitation features. Database access rules restrict those records to the appropriate signed-in user or room members.

External services

The site uses Supabase for authentication and application data, Cloudflare Pages for hosting and security delivery, Google for optional OAuth and Calendar links, Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap services for optional weather/location functions, and YouTube for public festival videos and radio content. Their respective privacy terms apply when you use those services.

Retention and your choices

You can use most public experiences without an account, deny location or contact access, and sign out at any time. To request access to or deletion of account data, contact sohamroy.pkt@gmail.com.

Security and children

Uma Boron uses HTTPS, browser security headers, rate limits, and database row-level access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features or service providers change. The updated date at the top will identify the current version.
